YOGURT
Substitutions:
- Buttermilk
- Coconut milk
- Cream

Use case:
Yogurt can be replaced with buttermilk in marinades and curries.
Coconut milk can be used when a creamy texture is required.
Cream can be used when richness is required.

EGG
Substitutions:
- Tofu
- Mashed potato
- Flaxseed mixture

Use case:
Tofu can replace egg in some savory dishes.
Mashed potato can help bind mixtures.
Flaxseed mixture can be used as a binding ingredient.

BUTTER
Substitutions:
- Oil
- Ghee
- Margarine

Use case:
Oil can replace butter for frying and cooking.
Ghee can replace butter when a richer flavor is desired.

MILK
Substitutions:
- Coconut milk
- Soy milk
- Oat milk

Use case:
Plant-based milk can replace regular milk in many recipes.

CHEESE
Substitutions:
- Paneer
- Tofu
- Nutritional yeast

Use case:
Paneer or tofu can provide a different protein-rich texture.
Nutritional yeast can provide a cheese-like savory flavor.

CREAM
Substitutions:
- Coconut milk
- Yogurt
- Milk

Use case:
Coconut milk can provide creaminess.
Yogurt can provide a tangy creamy texture.

POTATO
Substitutions:
- Sweet potato
- Cauliflower

Use case:
Sweet potato can replace potato in some curries.
Cauliflower can be used as a lower-starch alternative.

RICE
Substitutions:
- Quinoa
- Couscous

Use case:
Quinoa or couscous can replace rice in some dishes,
but the cooking method may need to be adjusted.

OIL
Substitutions:
- Butter
- Ghee

Use case:
Butter or ghee can be used for cooking when appropriate.

TOMATO
Substitutions:
- Tomato puree
- Tamarind
- Red bell pepper

Use case:
Tomato puree works well when tomato flavor is required.
Tamarind can provide acidity in curries.
Red bell pepper can provide sweetness and body.
