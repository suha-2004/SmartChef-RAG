CHICKEN CURRY

CATEGORY: Non-Vegetarian

INGREDIENTS:
Chicken
Onion
Tomato
Garlic
Ginger
Green chilli
Oil
Salt
Pepper
Chilli
Water

COOKING TIME: 40 minutes

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Heat oil in a pan.
2. Add chopped onion and cook until golden brown.
3. Add garlic, ginger and green chilli.
4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
5. Add chicken, salt, pepper and chilli.
6. Mix everything well and cook the chicken.
7. Add water and simmer until the chicken is completely cooked.
8. Serve hot with rice or bread.

TIPS:
Marinate the chicken with salt and chilli before cooking for better flavour.
