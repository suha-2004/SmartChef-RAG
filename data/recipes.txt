CHICKEN FRIED RICE

CATEGORY: Non-Vegetarian
CUISINE: Chinese
TYPE: Main Course

INGREDIENTS:
Chicken
Rice
Onion
Carrot
Capsicum
Green peas
Garlic
Soy sauce
Oil
Salt
Pepper

COOKING TIME: 25 minutes

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Cook the rice and keep it aside.
2. Heat oil in a pan.
3. Add garlic, onion, carrot, capsicum and peas.
4. Add chicken and cook completely.
5. Add cooked rice and soy sauce.
6. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Mix well and serve hot.

TIPS:
Use chilled cooked rice for better fried rice.
